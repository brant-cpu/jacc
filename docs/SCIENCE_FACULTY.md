## Teaching and access

Each professor gives a brief purpose and offers two existing repeatable activities. **PIP, show the next step** opens one small optional instruction and the matching activity. Professor = subject instructor; PIP = continuous active helper. Directions can be repeated/read aloud. There are no required lessons, quizzes, time limits, grades or forced character switches.

In authored environments, use the pictorial professor guide in the reserved footer. This opens a conversation/communication panel rather than creating a roaming NPC. In generated exploration worlds and colonies, use **Tools / trails → Professor**; the header PIP and PIP-goal controls still work. These locations keep the new controls clear of existing destinations and navigation. Every professor panel includes **Meet the other professors**, with a paged directory of all twelve. The HQ science-lab card also links to the directory, and the crystal-lookout card links to Iris. Biology, optics and mathematics use the existing science resources rather than claiming new standalone lab scenes.

Activities reuse farm, geology, aquatic, magnetism, computer/robotics, rocket, quartz-analysis and PIP-tool models. Viewing a PIP lesson through a professor never replaces the child's saved PIP tool or changes a suit's abilities. Hypothetical alien conditions remain distinct from confirmed life; no extraterrestrial life is claimed. Scientific model limits and existing source-review notes still apply.

Shared data/portraits/routing are in `prototype/src/professors.js`; browser adapters are in `prototype/src/main.js`, native adapters in `gdevelop/src/professors.js`. The existing game objects and destination counts are retained. Tests cover zone assignments, all twelve first-activity links, PIP handoffs, native portraits, repeatable models and unchanged saved rooms/PIP fittings. This is the instructor/content framework, not conversational AI, autonomous NPC AI or final voice acting. Real-device, editor and accessibility user testing remain pending.
