## Orange J.A.C.C.

The seventh J.A.C.C. is a girl from India. Her suit is orange. The Gadgil Model honors Indian ecologist Madhav Gadgil and his contributions to ecology and biodiversity conservation.

Her scientific focus is relationships among living things and their surroundings: ecosystems, food webs, pollination, biodiversity, conservation, habitat restoration, and connections with water, soil and air. Keep this specialization separate from Lucas's biology and xenobiology focus on organisms, biological processes and possible life beyond Earth.

Her personal name has not been selected. This approved character concept is recorded here; adding her to both playable crew menus, PIP color choices and character assets remains implementation work. Do not invent a permanent name, biography, suit design or special ability to fill the gaps.
