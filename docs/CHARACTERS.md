## Sumati Suri — Orange J.A.C.C.

Sumati Suri, the seventh J.A.C.C., is a girl from India. Her suit is orange. The Gadgil Model honors Indian ecologist Madhav Gadgil and his contributions to ecology and biodiversity conservation.

Her scientific focus is relationships among living things and their surroundings: ecosystems, food webs, pollination, biodiversity, conservation, habitat restoration, and connections with water, soil and air. Keep this specialization separate from Lucas's biology and xenobiology focus on organisms, biological processes and possible life beyond Earth.

Sumati is selectable in both builds and has a matching PIP color. Her visuals are temporary suit-color placeholders. Do not invent additional biography or permanent suit artwork.

## Lilja Jónsdóttir — Purple J.A.C.C.

Lilja is a female math major from Iceland. Her purple suit uses the Mirzakhani Model — Mathematics, honoring mathematician Maryam Mirzakhani. Her focus includes patterns, geometry, symmetry, measurement and probability. She is selectable in both builds and has a matching PIP color. Visuals are temporary suit-color placeholders.

## PIP science attachments

All crew members can fit every attachment. Life Lens and Xeno Lab support Lucas’s biology/xenobiology; Eco Probe supports Sumati’s ecosystems/environmental science; Pattern Projector supports Lilja’s mathematics. They provide three repeatable illustrated steps each. Fictional alien life is labeled imaginary, and conditions suitable for life are distinguished from confirmed organisms. These tools are schematic teaching models, not new character-exclusive abilities.

## Astronaut identity and suit powers

Every J.A.C.C. is an astronaut with a scientific specialization. Lucas Turner is French (France); his name, black suit and Lederberg specialization remain unchanged.

Lucas now has Bio-Vision, Adaptation Scan and Xeno Analysis; Sumati has Connection Vision, Environmental Survey and Restoration Planner; Lilja has Pattern Vision, Measure & Map and Geometry Builder. Their current implementations are one-tap contextual overlays and repeatable illustrated training models, with actual map measurements and farm/construction links. See [suit implementation and limits](SUIT_POWERS.md).

## Shared suit material and energy colors

Approved across every astronaut suit: **silver for all metal accents** and **teal for all lights, thrusters and energy effects**. These are shared design elements, not additional primary suit colors. The ninth astronaut’s primary suit color is **blue**. Configurable silver/teal placeholder swatches are recorded as `suitFinish` in `prototype/src/data.js`; production artwork and existing provisional effects still need a visual pass to apply this standard consistently.
