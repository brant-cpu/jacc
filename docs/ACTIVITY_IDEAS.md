## Proposed 2D biome learning framework

Primary placement: a biome comparison station in the existing Planet Farm biodome. It connects to Sumati's ecosystems/environmental science tools, Lucas's organism/adaptation tools and future My World/My Solar System planning. A separate biome region is not yet being added.

The supplied screenshots list seven biome categories: Rainforest, Grassland, Coniferous Forest, Temperate Deciduous Forest, Desert, Tundra and Shrubland. This records the displayed classification, not an exhaustive or universal biome classification.

- Tap a habitat to explore an illustrated 2D scene.
- Observe model climate patterns, water availability, vegetation and animal adaptations. Explain that biomes reflect long-term conditions; one rainy day does not change a desert into a forest.
- Compare two model settings using discrete taps, with PIP explaining changes. Avoid presenting simplified diagrams as ecological predictions.
- Keep wildlife observations in the journal without capturing animals or requiring quizzes.
- Connect farm/habitat engineering to appropriate conditions. Future fictional worlds must distinguish Earth examples, speculative environments and evidence for life.

Sumati studies relationships among organisms and surroundings; Lucas studies organisms and biological adaptations. All astronauts can use the activities.

## Activities verified from the supplied screenshots

**The Great Graph Match:** the screenshot asks players to help Geo Grapher match temperature and precipitation graphs for different locations to the appropriate biomes.

Proposed J.A.C.C. adaptation: **Climate Detective**, at the farm biodome climate station, with a matching display in the GSEINHQ mathematics area. Lilja helps interpret graphs; Sumati connects climate patterns to ecosystems. Select a graph card, hear an optional description, then select a biome. Use monthly temperature/precipitation patterns, explicit axes/units, picture cues and plain-language summaries. Offer comparison, PIP demonstration and repeat instead of required testing or scores. Do not require precise graph tapping or rely solely on colors.

**To Plant, or Not to Plant:** the screenshot asks players to help Bill Botanist place plants in the correct biome.

Proposed J.A.C.C. adaptation: **Find My Growing Place**, at the farm biodome/plant computer. Select a plant, then select a model growing environment. Sumati explains its environmental relationships and Lucas its biological adaptations. Use scientifically reviewed species and suitable-condition ranges; avoid implying every species belongs exclusively to one biome. Explain choices through observation and repeatable growing models, not plant death or punishment. Link suitable plant records to the existing seed bank/journal when implemented.

The screenshots also show a color-coded world biome map. Our proposed biome selector should add habitat pictures, names, patterns and spoken descriptions, with large buttons as an alternative to selecting small map regions. Retain existing color-blind/high-contrast support.

Use J.A.C.C.'s own characters and original artwork. NASA's pictured characters, illustrations and map have not been imported or cleared for reuse. Neither proposed adaptation is implemented yet.

## Other proposed 2D science stations

Spacecraft assembly (engineering), rover observation tasks (future planetary training), crater comparisons (dig site), Moon-phase geometry (GSEINHQ astronomy), illustrated spectra (science lab), water-cycle exploration (existing farm/aquatic models), and icy-ocean-moon cutaways (aquatic center). These are original adaptation proposals, not verified extracts from the blocked NASA pages.

Every new activity should retain large controls, no required dragging/timing, repeat/demonstrate/undo where applicable, optional narration/captions, switch/controller access and an ungated route back to exploration. Production science review and real-device/child testing remain necessary.
