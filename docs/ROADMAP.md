# J.A.C.C. progression and development order

Granite Pixel Games. Direction supplied by the project owner; implementation status below is explicit.

## Current playable foundation

- Training grounds are the home hub, connecting HQ, farm, side-view excavation and the construction yard and material workbenches.
- Discover and keep rock, mineral and fossil samples. Aiden's user-specified drill, scoop and power-arm role gives him faster excavation; PIP assists the other astronauts with hard formations.
- Starter bridge-support, water-channel and protected-habitat designs can reference collected samples and install model modules at the farm/greenhouse. Samples remain reusable; fossils are preserved for observation.
- Farm seeds, plant library, experiments, journal, crew and PIP customization already exist.

## Engineering and construction zone — playable prototype

The yard now includes a 3D-printer lesson, crane and robot demonstrations, six side-profile prefab challenges and a free-build sandbox. Pipe connectivity, a closed valve, complete circuit, intended supports, undo, repeat and robot demonstrations are implemented. Nine reusable kits can be fitted at the farm. These are rule-based models rather than a real engineering/physics simulation.

The home-hub elevator project now has an earned three-stage training model and animated orbit preview. The full giant elevator, real-scale tether/station travel and generated/editable My Solar System endgame are still future milestones. Further work can expand construction variety, material processing, larger settlements and operation of built equipment on generated worlds.

## Later: space travel, worlds and colonies

- Build a rocket pad and accessible launch/travel flow.
- Generate repeatable rocky and aquatic worlds from saved seeds. Keep generated layouts navigable with one finger, with bounded resources and predictable restoration.
- Establish farms, greenhouse/habitat modules and colonies. Treat whole-planet terraforming as a long-term fictional game system, separate from realistic small-habitat gardening.
- **My World:** keep and revisit a child's chosen world and its discoveries/builds.
- **My Solar System:** assemble and retain a miniature system or group of colonies as the endgame.
- Before implementation, design stable world identifiers, save-version migrations, local save/export and recovery. Native previews now attempt host-local storage, with origin/availability limits; no cloud/account/save service exists. My World/My Solar System editors remain plans; only the earned training orbit preview is playable.

## Later: Adventure Mode on other worlds

Separate arcade adventures from peaceful exploration/building. The owner requested laser encounters with hostile robots and fictional creatures, with a rail-shooter option and/or tap-to-move/tap-to-blast play inspired by accessible arcade interaction. Keep large targets and forgiving aiming. No simultaneous movement/firing controls or mandatory rapid tapping. Use the approved J.A.C.C. models; do not invent permanent character powers or biographies without direction. Decide challenge, assist settings and age-appropriate presentation when designing this mode.

No combat, enemies, rocket travel, procedural planets, colonies or solar-system simulation are implemented in the current slice. Cavern animals remain observation subjects, not opponents or crafting materials.
